Information we collect

When you submit a travel inquiry, payment inquiry, newsletter signup, or contact request, Huddle Up N Travel may collect information you provide such as your name, email address, phone number, destination preferences, travel dates, number of travelers, budget range, and trip details.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to requests, plan or discuss travel services, provide trip and payment guidance, communicate about Huddle Up N Travel offerings, and operate and improve the website.

Forms and hosting

This website is hosted on Netlify. Form submissions may be processed through Netlify Forms and stored in Netlify's systems so Huddle Up N Travel can respond to your request.

Payment information

Huddle Up N Travel does not request or store complete payment-card details through ordinary website forms. Card payments should be completed only through an approved payment processor or verified payment destination provided by Huddle Up N Travel.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers or travel suppliers when reasonably necessary to fulfill a request or support website operations. We do not sell personal information through this website.
